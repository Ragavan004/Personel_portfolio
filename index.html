01 — About

Who I Am

I'm Ragavan N, an M.Sc. Computer Science postgraduate from Central University of Tamil Nadu, Thiruvarur (2026), passionate about building intelligent AI systems that understand human language and visual context.

My core interest lies at the intersection of Natural Language Processing, Computer Vision, and Large Language Models — particularly for low-resource Dravidian languages like Tamil, where most of the world's AI advances remain inaccessible.

I have hands-on experience fine-tuning state-of-the-art transformer models including MuRIL, mBERT, CLIP, and BLIP-2 and building multimodal pipelines from raw social media data to production-ready evaluation.

My career goal is to become a full-stack AI/NLP engineer — solving real-world problems through research-driven engineering and contributing meaningfully to the development of inclusive multilingual AI.
